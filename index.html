01 / ABOUT

Research driven by curiosity.

I work at the intersection of machine learning, audio processing, and real-world sensing. My research explores how AI models can interpret complex acoustic environments and how these capabilities can be adapted to practical applications.

Beyond individual model performance, I am interested in understanding model behaviour, improving robustness under real-world conditions, and translating research into practical systems.

I enjoy interdisciplinary research, learning new technologies, and connecting ideas across different research domains.

02 / RESEARCH

Research interests

Audio AI & Speech Processing

Automatic speech recognition, speaker diarization, audio tagging, and acoustic scene classification in challenging environments.

Robust & Adaptive Machine Learning

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning, bottleneck adapters, LoRA, and domain adaptation for robust audio models.

Pervasive Health & Mobile Sensing

On-device audio intelligence, smartphone sensing, and the analysis of everyday activities using machine learning.

Explainable AI

Interpreting model predictions and identifying meaningful relationships between acoustic features, semantic events, and model decisions.
